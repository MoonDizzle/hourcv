Questions

Does it really arrive within the hour?

Yes, timed from when you send the questionnaire. Our test orders take under five minutes. If yours takes more than 60, half your payment is refunded automatically.

What if I don't like it?

Reply to the delivery email with what you want changed. Two rounds are included. If after both rounds you still don't want it, ask for a refund within 7 days and you'll get one.

Will it pass applicant tracking systems?

It's built for them: single column, standard section names, consistent dates, no tables or graphics, and the advert's own wording where you genuinely match it. No service can promise what a particular employer's software will do, and anyone who does is guessing.

Do you use my CV for anything else?

No. It's used to write yours and deleted after 30 days.
