Privacy

We collect your name, email, your CV and your questionnaire answers so that we can write your documents and send them to you. Payment details go to Stripe; we never see your card number. Forms and records are stored in Airtable; email is sent through Google Workspace. Your CV text is processed by an AI model (Anthropic Claude) to draft and check your documents; it is not used to train anything.

We keep your files for 30 days after delivery so we can handle revisions, then delete them. Free CV score users get up to two follow-up emails; reply "stop" to end them at any time.

You can ask for a copy or deletion of your data at any time: daniel@transmutemedia.org. Controller: Daniel Mooney, Transmute Media.
